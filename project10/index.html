Cricket

Cricket is a popular bat-and-ball team sport played between two teams of eleven players on a large field with a 22-yard pitch at its center

Origin

Cricket was invented in England, specifically during the medieval period in the southeast region of the country

Invented Year

Cricket was invented in southeast England, 
            with its origins dating back as a children's game possibly as early as Saxon or
             Norman times (the medieval period) and definitely by the late 16th century

Formats in Cricket

International cricket is played in three main formats governed by the International Cricket Council (ICC): Test matches, One-Day Internationals (ODIs), and Twenty20 Internationals (T20Is)

ODI Matches

A One Day International (ODI) cricket match lasts about 7 to 8 hours and consists of a single innings per side with a maximum of 50 overs per team

Test Matches

A Test match is the oldest and most serious type of international cricket gameStandard Test match cricket sessions usually run for six hours of play per day, typically starting at 11:00 AM local time
