1. pizza

    • Pizza is a savory dish made of a flattened disk of bread dough topped with tomatoes, cheese, and various other ingredients, then baked at a high temperature

    • Essential pizza ingredients include the dough components (flour, yeast, water, salt, and olive oil), the sauce base (tomatoes, garlic, and herbs), and your choice of cheese and toppings

      1. Prepare the Oven and Pan
        • Turn your oven on very hot, around 450°F to 475°F (230°C to 245°C).
        • Put a baking sheet or pizza pan inside so it gets hot.
      2. Shape the Dough
        • Dust your hands and a clean counter with flour.
        • Gently press and stretch your pizza dough out into a flat round circle using your fingers. Do not use a rolling pin if you want a soft, airy crust.
      3. Add the Sauce
        • Place your flattened dough on a piece of baking paper or a greased tray.
        • Spoon a thin, even layer of tomato sauce over the middle, leaving a small empty edge for the crust.
      4. Add Cheese and Toppings
        • Sprinkle a handful of shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
        • Add your favorite toppings like sliced vegetables or meats, but keep them light so the pizza does not get soggy
      5. Bake and Serve
        • Carefully put the pizza into the hot oven.
        • Bake for 10 to 15 minutes until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly.
        • Carefully put the pizza into the hot oven!.

    • Timings:

    • Morning : 9 am - 1 pm
    • Evening : 4 pm - 11pm

    • Servings

    • A standard large 14-inch pizza serves 3 to 4 adults, yielding about 8 slices with an average of 2 to 3 slices per person

    • Tips

    • Make better homemade pizza by using a digital scale to weigh ingredients, cranking your oven as hot as it will go, and keeping your toppings light.
